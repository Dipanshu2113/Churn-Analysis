📊 Customer Churn Analysis & Prediction – Power BI
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer churn, identify high-risk customers, and generate actionable business insights for improving customer retention.
🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall customer churn and churn rate
Identify high-churn customer segments
Analyze churn by age, gender, state, tenure, contract, and payment method
Understand churn across different services
Identify major churn categories/reasons
Highlight customers predicted to be at risk of churning
📌 Key Dashboard Metrics
Total Customers: 6,418
New Joiners: 411
Total Churn: 1,732
Churn Rate: 27.0%
Predicted Churners: 378

📊 Dashboard Pages
1. Churn Analysis – Summary
Provides an overall view of customer churn across demographics, geography, payment methods, contracts, tenure, and services.
2. Churn Analysis – Prediction
Highlights predicted churners with customer-level details and analyzes their characteristics by state, age, gender, tenure, payment method, and contract.
💡 Key Insights
Month-to-Month contract customers have the highest churn.
Jammu & Kashmir has the highest churn rate among the displayed states.
Competitor-related reasons contribute significantly to customer churn.
Short-tenure customers represent an important churn-risk segment.
The prediction page helps identify 378 customers at risk.
